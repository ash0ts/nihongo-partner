# The 6-Hour Genki I (Lessons 0–2) Cram Playbook

Built for walking into a **Genki I Lesson 3–4** class cold and keeping up from minute one.

---

## 0. The One Rule That Governs Your 6 Hours

Genki I prints romaji under every Japanese sentence in Lessons 1 and 2. **On page 1 of Lesson 3, romaji disappears completely.** Every sentence switches to kanji with small hiragana readings (`furigana`) above them, plus katakana loanwords (`コーヒー`, `テレビ`, `デート`, `テニス`).

That gives you a clean engineering priority order for today:

1. **Zero handwriting drills from memory today.** Learning to recognize 92 kana characters takes ~90 minutes with visual mnemonics and contrast pairs. Learning to recall stroke order from blank paper triples the time and buys almost nothing on night one.
2. **Compress grammar and numbers into rules + bounded exceptions.** Japanese has zero subject-verb person conjugation, zero noun gender, and zero articles (`a`/`the`). Almost every beginner mistake comes from ~15 predictable phonetic and particle traps listed below.
3. **Kill the romaji crutch at Hour 5.** Spend your last two hours reading in **L3 Class Mode (Kanji + Furigana, No Romaji)** in the workbench.

### 60-Second macOS Japanese Keyboard Setup (Do This Before Hour 1)

1. Open **System Settings -> Keyboard -> Text Input -> Input Sources -> Edit...**
2. Click **`+` -> Japanese -> Japanese - Romaji -> Add**.
3. While typing: press `Ctrl + Shift + J` for **Hiragana**, `Ctrl + Shift + K` for **Katakana**, and `Ctrl + Shift + ;` (or `Caps Lock`) to return to English ASCII.
4. Type phonetically (`watashi` -> `わたし`). Two keyboard quirks to know immediately:
   - Double a consonant (`kk`, `pp`, `tt`, `ss`) to get small `っ` (`chotto` -> `ちょっと`, `roppyaku` -> `ろっぴゃく`).
   - Type `nn` before a vowel or at the end of a word to lock in `ん` (`nihonn` -> `にほん`), and type `wo` for the object particle `を`.

## 1. Hour-by-Hour Cram Schedule

- **Hour 1 (0:00–1:00) — Hiragana Reading Blitz:** Scan the 46 base characters down the 5-vowel columns, learn the 4 voicing switches (`゛`/`゜`) + small `ゃ/ゅ/ょ` + small `っ`, then drill the 5 Hiragana lookalike groups (`は/ほ`, `さ/き`, `ね/れ/わ`, `ぬ/め`, `る/ろ`) until >= 90% accuracy.
- **Hour 2 (1:00–2:00) — Katakana + Real L1–L2 Loanwords:** Lock in the `シ/ン` vs `ツ/ソ` stroke-angle rule, `ウ/ワ/フ`, `マ/ム/ア`, and long-vowel dash `ー`. Immediately decode the 18 Lesson 1–2 katakana vocab words (`アメリカ`, `イギリス`, `コンピューター`, `ビジネス`, `スマホ`, `コンビニ`, `メニュー`, `トイレ`).
- **Hour 3 (2:00–3:00) — Greetings Register & Lesson 1 Syntax:** Master `[Topic は] Y です`, subject omission, `〜ですか` questions, right-headed `Noun1 の Noun2` chains, and rehearse your 5-sentence Japanese self-introduction aloud.
- **Hour 4 (3:00–4:00) — Numbers, 10^4 `まん`, Time, Age & Yen Lab:** Memorize ONLY the phonetic shifts (`300/600/800`, `3000/8000`, `10,000 = いちまん`, `4:00 よじ`, `7:00 しちじ`, `9:00 くじ`, `-ぷん` on `1/3/4/6/8/10`, `4th year = よねんせい`, `20 yrs = はたち`) and run 20 rapid-fire prompts.
- **Hour 5 (4:00–5:00) — Lesson 2 Pointers (`こそあど`), `も`, `じゃないです`, `ね/よ` (No-Romaji Mode!):** Turn romaji OFF in the app. Drill `これ` vs `この+N` vs `ここ/そこ/あそこ`, `だれの+N`, `も` replacing `は`, `じゃないです`, and `ね` vs `よ`.
- **Hour 6 (5:00–6:00) — Page 81 Classroom Survival, Roleplay & Lesson 3 Preview:** Lock in spoken classroom recovery lines (`ゆっくり いってください`, `もういちど いってください`, `わかりません`), run the self-intro and shopping/restaurant roleplays, and spend 15 minutes skimming the Lesson 3 verb + particle preview at the bottom of this guide.

## 2. The Sound & Script System (Hiragana + Katakana)

Japanese has **5 pure, non-diphthong vowels**:
- `あ / ア (a)` — "ah" as in *father*
- `い / イ (i)` — "ee" as in *machine*
- `う / ウ (u)` — unrounded "oo" as in *flute* (lips relaxed, not pushed forward)
- `え / エ (e)` — "eh" as in *bet*
- `お / オ (o)` — crisp "oh" as in *coat* (no trailing "w" glide)

Every character (including standalone `ん/ン` and small pause `っ/ッ`) takes **one equal rhythmic beat** (*mora*). `とうきょう` (`to-u-kyo-u`) is 4 beats; `きって` (`ki-t-te`, stamp) is 3 beats vs `きて` (`ki-te`, please come) at 2 beats.

### 5x10 Base Kana Coordinate Grid (Hiragana / Katakana)

- **Vowels:** `あ/ア (a)` · `い/イ (i)` · `う/ウ (u)` · `え/エ (e)` · `お/オ (o)`
- **K-row:** `か/カ (ka)` · `き/キ (ki)` · `く/ク (ku)` · `け/ケ (ke)` · `こ/コ (ko)`
- **S-row:** `さ/サ (sa)` · **`し/シ (shi)`** · `す/ス (su)` · `せ/セ (se)` · `そ/ソ (so)`
- **T-row:** `た/タ (ta)` · **`ち/チ (chi)`** · **`つ/ツ (tsu)`** · `て/テ (te)` · `と/ト (to)`
- **N-row:** `な/ナ (na)` · `に/ニ (ni)` · `ぬ/ヌ (nu)` · `ね/ネ (ne)` · `の/ノ (no)`
- **H-row:** `は/ハ (ha / topic wa)` · `ひ/ヒ (hi)` · **`ふ/フ (fu)`** · `へ/ヘ (he / dir e)` · `ほ/ホ (ho)`
- **M-row:** `ま/マ (ma)` · `み/ミ (mi)` · `む/ム (mu)` · `め/メ (me)` · `も/モ (mo)`
- **Y-row:** `や/ヤ (ya)` · *(no yi)* · `ゆ/ユ (yu)` · *(no ye)* · `よ/ヨ (yo)`
- **R-row:** `ら/ラ (ra)` · `り/リ (ri)` · `る/ル (ru)` · `れ/レ (re)` · `ろ/ロ (ro)` *(light single tap on roof of mouth, halfway between English L and R)*
- **W-row + N:** `わ/ワ (wa)` · **`を/ヲ (o — object particle only)`** · **`ん/ン (n)`**

### 4 Voicing Rules (Unlocks 25 More Characters Instantly)

- **K + `゛` -> G:** `が/ガ (ga), ぎ/ギ (gi), ぐ/グ (gu), げ/ゲ (ge), ご/ゴ (go)`
- **S + `゛` -> Z (except `ji`):** `ざ/ザ (za),` **`じ/ジ (ji)`**`, ず/ズ (zu), ぜ/ゼ (ze), ぞ/ゾ (zo)`
- **T + `゛` -> D (except `ji/zu`):** `だ/ダ (da), ぢ/ヂ (ji), づ/ヅ (zu), で/デ (de), ど/ド (do)`
- **H + `゛` -> B / H + `゜` -> P:** `ば..ぼ / バ..ボ (ba..bo)` and `ぱ..ぽ / パ..ポ (pa..po)`

### Three Orthography Mechanics

1. **Glides (`きゃ, しゃ, ちゃ, りゅう, しょ...`):** Any `-i` column character + **small** `ゃ / ゅ / ょ` merges into a single 1-beat sound (`りゅうがくせい` = `ryuu-ga-ku-se-i`, `としょかん` = `to-sho-ka-n`).
2. **Double Consonant Stop (`っ / ッ`):** Small `つ/ツ` holds a 1-beat silent stop before the next consonant (`いっぷん` = `ip-pun`, `ざっし` = `zas-shi`).
3. **Long Vowels:**
   - Hiragana extends `o` with **`う`** (`せんこう`, `おとうさん`, `おはよう`; rare `おお` exceptions: `おおきい`, `おおさか`) and extends `e` with **`い`** (`せんせい`, `がくせい`, `えいご`; rare `ええ` exceptions: `おねえさん`, `ええ`).
   - Katakana extends **every** long vowel with **`ー`** (`コーヒー`, `コンピューター`, `メニュー`, `ノート`).

### High-Confusion Lookalike Geometry Cheats

- **`シ (shi)` & `ン (n)` vs `ツ (tsu)` & `ソ (so)`:**
  - `シ` and `ン` have **horizontal** left ticks and their long stroke sweeps **UP-right from the bottom** (just like Hiragana `し` hooks up-right).
  - `ツ` and `ソ` have **vertical** top ticks and their long stroke drops **DOWN-left from the top** (just like Hiragana `つ` starts across the top and drops down).
  - Test word with both: `Ｔシャツ` (`tii-sha-tsu` -> first is `シ`+`ャ`, last is `ツ`) and `パソコン` (`pa-so-ko-n` -> second is `ソ`, last is `ン`).
- **`は (ha)` vs `ほ (ho)`:** `は` has **1** horizontal bar on the right; `ほ` has **2** (top bar forms a flat Santa roof: "ho-ho-ho").
- **`さ (sa)` vs `き (ki)`:** `さ` has **1** crossbar; `き` has **2** teeth like a house key.
- **`ね (ne)` vs `れ (re)` vs `わ (wa)`:** Left post is identical; check the bottom-right tail! `ね` loops into a sleeping cat (`neko`) tail, `れ` kicks outward, `わ` sweeps smoothly inward.
- **`ぬ (nu)` vs `め (me)` & `る (ru)` vs `ろ (ro)`:** `ぬ` has a noodle loop (`め` has none); `る` has a ruby loop (`ろ` has none).
- **`ウ (u)` vs `ワ (wa)` vs `フ (fu)`:** `ウ` = top tick + left wall + curve; `ワ` = no top tick; `フ` = no top tick and no left wall.

## 3. Syntax & Grammar Blueprint (Lessons 1 & 2)

### Core Sentence Engine

```text
[Topic + は / も]   [Modifier + の + Head Noun]   [です / じゃないです]   [か / ね / よ]
  (drop when clear!)     (right-headed noun!)       (copula at end!)     (mood flag!)
```

1. **Zero-Subject Default (`L1.1`):** Once the topic is clear, drop `わたしは`!
   - Q: `せんこうは なんですか。` (What is [your] major?)
   - Natural A: `コンピューターです。` (Computer science.)
2. **Topic Particle `は` (`L1.1`):** Written `は` (`ha`), pronounced `wa`. Introduces or contrasts what you're talking about ("As for X...").Note: `です` has a whispered/silent final *u* (`dess`), and `ます` sounds like `mass`.
3. **Polite Names (`〜さん / 〜せんせい`) & Avoiding `あなた` (`L1 Note`):**
   - Never attach `〜さん` to your own name (`わたしは アニッシュです`).
   - Never call a teacher `〜さん` (always `〜せんせい`).
   - Avoid `あなた` ("you"): address classmates by `<Name>さん` (`ミナさんは なんねんせいですか`).
4. **Questions (`〜ですか`) & `なん` vs `なに` (`L1.2`):**
   - Zero word-order inversion! Keep statement order and add `か` with rising intonation: `ミナさんは がくせいですか。`
   - Wh-words sit in the exact slot the answer occupies: `いま [なんじ] ですか` -> `いま [よじ] です`.
   - Use **`なん`** before `t/d/n` sounds and counters (`なんですか`, `なんじ`, `なんさい`, `なんねんせい`, `なんばん`); use `なに` before other particles (`なにを...` in L3).
5. **Right-Headed Noun Chaining (`Noun1 の Noun2`, `L1.3`):**
   - The **final noun** is always the main object; everything before `の` narrows or owns it:
     - `わたしの でんわばんごう` = my phone number
     - `とうきょうだいがくの がくせい` = student at the University of Tokyo
     - `にほんごの せんせい` = Japanese-language teacher (vs `にほんじんの せんせい` = teacher who is Japanese)
     - `これは だれの かさですか` (`L2.4`) = Whose umbrella is this? -> `わたしの (かさ) です` = It's mine.
6. **The 4x3 `こそあど` Spatial Pointer Matrix (`L2.1–L2.3`):**
   - **`こ` (near speaker / me):** `これ` (standalone "this one") · `この + Noun` ("this [noun]") · `ここ` ("here")
   - **`そ` (near listener / you):** `それ` (standalone "that one near you") · `その + Noun` ("that [noun]") · `そこ` ("there near you")
   - **`あ` (far from both):** `あれ` (standalone "that one over there") · `あの + Noun` ("that [noun] over there") · **`あそこ`** ("over there" — **irregular!**)
   - **`ど` (question, 3+ items):** `どれ` ("which one") · `どの + Noun` ("which [noun]") · `どこ` ("where")
   - **Hard rule:** `これ/それ/あれ/どれ` **never** precede a noun (`× これほん` -> `○ このほん`). And question words (`どれ`, `どこ`, `だれ`) never take topic `は` (`× どれは...` -> `○ あなたのペンは どれですか`).
7. **Inclusive `も` ("also / too", `L2.5`):**
   - Completely **replaces** `は` (`× ソラさんはも...` -> `○ けんさんは にねんせいです。ソラさんも にねんせいです。`).
8. **Present Negative Copula (`Noun じゃないです`, `L2.6`):**
   - Conversational polite negative taught first in Genki 3rd Edition: `さかなじゃないです` ("It is not fish"). More formal variants you will also hear: `じゃありません` / `ではありません`.
9. **Sentence-Ending `ね` vs `よ` (`L2.7`) & Register Ordering:**
   - **`ね`** = shared observation / seeking confirmation ("right? / isn't it?"): `きょうは いい てんきですね` / `このとけいは たかいですね`.
   - **`よ`** = new info the listener doesn't know ("heads up / I assure you!"): `とんかつは さかなじゃないですよ。にくですよ。`
   - Purchasing / ordering: `[Item] を ください` ("Please give me [item]") or `[Item] を おねがいします` ("[Item], please").

## 4. Numbers, \(10^4\) `まん`, Time, Age & Yen Exceptions

### Base 0–10

`0 ゼロ/れい` · `1 いち` · `2 に` · `3 さん` · `4 よん (し)` · `5 ご` · `6 ろく` · `7 なな (しち)` · `8 はち` · `9 きゅう (く)` · `10 じゅう`
*(11–99 are 100% regular: `24 = にじゅうよん`, `99 = きゅうじゅうきゅう`.)*

### Big Numbers & Yen Prices (Memorize ONLY the 5 Shifts + `まん` Grouping)

- **Hundreds (`ひゃく`):**
  - `300 =` **`さんびゃく`** (`sanbyaku`)
  - `600 =` **`ろっぴゃく`** (`roppyaku`)
  - `800 =` **`はっぴゃく`** (`happyaku`)
- **Thousands (`せん`):**
  - `1,000 = せん` (standalone)
  - `3,000 =` **`さんぜん`** (`sanzen`)
  - `8,000 =` **`はっせん`** (`hassen`)
- **Ten-Thousands (`まん` = \(10^4\)):**
  - Japanese regroups every **four** digits (`1,0000`), not three!
  - `10,000 =` **`いちまん`** (requires `いち`! Never bare `まん`)
  - `38,000円 = さんまん はっせんえん` (`3 × 10,000 + 8,000`)
  - `100,000円 = じゅうまんえん` (`10 × 10,000`)

### Clock Time (`ごぜん` AM / `ごご` PM + Hour `〜じ` + Minute `〜ふん/ぷん`)

- **3 Irregular Hours:** **`4:00 = よじ`** (NEVER `よんじ`!), **`7:00 = しちじ`**, **`9:00 = くじ`** (NEVER `きゅうじ`!).
- **Minutes (`1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10` -> `-ぷん`; `2, 5, 7, 9` -> `-ふん`):**
  - `1分 いっぷん` · `2分 にふん` · `3分 さんぷん` · `4分 よんぷん` · `5分 ごふん`
  - `6分 ろっぷん` · `7分 ななふん` · `8分 はっぷん (or はちふん)` · `9分 きゅうふん` · `10分 じゅっぷん (じっぷん)`
  - `15分 じゅうごふん` · `20分 にじゅっぷん` · `30分 はん` (`にじはん` = 2:30)
- **AM / PM order:** `ごぜん` / `ごご` always come **before** the hour (`ごご よじはん` = 4:30 PM).

### Age (`〜さい`), School Year (`〜ねんせい`) & Phone (`の` + `〜ばん`)

- **School Year Trap:** `1st いちねんせい`, `2nd にねんせい`, `3rd さんねんせい`, **`4th よねんせい`** (drops `ん`!).
- **Age Traps:** `1 いっさい`, `8 はっさい`, `10 じゅっさい/じっさい`, **`20 はたち`**! Question: `なんさいですか` (polite: `おいくつですか`).
- **Phone Numbers:** Read `-` as **`の`** and append `ばん` if desired: `090-1234-5678` -> `ゼロきゅうゼロの いちにさんよんの ごろくななはちです`.

## 6. 10-Minute Preview of Tonight's Lesson 3 ("Making a Date")

Walking in knowing these 3 ideas puts you ahead of the room immediately:

1. **Verbs replace `です` at the very end in polite `〜ます` (present/future) and `〜ません` (negative):**
   - Ru-verbs drop `-ru` + `ます`: `たべる (eat) -> たべます`, `みる (watch) -> みます`, `おきる (wake up) -> おきます`, `ねる (sleep) -> ねます`.
   - U-verbs shift final `-u` vowel to `-i` + `ます`: `のむ (drink) -> のみます`, `いく (go) -> いきます`, `よむ (read) -> よみます`, `はなす (speak) -> はなします`, `かえる (return home — trap u-verb!) -> かえります`.
   - 2 Irregulars: `する (do) -> します` (`べんきょうします` = study), `くる (come) -> きます`.
2. **Four Action Particles tag every noun before the verb:**
   - **`を` (pronounced `o`):** direct object (`コーヒーを のみます` = drink coffee).
   - **`で`:** place where an action happens (`としょかんで べんきょうします` = study at the library).
   - **`に`:** destination (`がっこうに いきます`) OR **specific clock/day time** (`しちじに おきます` = wake up at 7:00; note relative words `きょう` today / `あした` tomorrow / `まいにち` every day take **NO `に`**!).
   - **`へ` (pronounced `e`):** direction toward a destination (`にほんへ いきます`).
3. **Polite Invitations (`〜ませんか`):**
   - `いっしょに ひるごはんを たべませんか。` = "Won't you eat lunch together?" -> Accept with `ええ、いいですね！` ("Sounds great!").
